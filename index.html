The project

StakePouch is a self-custody DeFi yield product built for the QMS Testnet.

Stake QMS or USDC on QMS Testnet, receive a staking receipt, and earn continuous yield — all while keeping full control of your keys. No middlemen. No custodial risk. Every action is signed in your wallet before it runs.

Designed for the post-quantum era of QMS, StakePouch keeps the experience simple: connect, stake, earn. Transparent APY, live positions, and explorer-linked transactions so you can verify every step.

Staking pairs

QMS & USDC on QMS Testnet.

Two pools are live on the testnet: stake QMS or USDC. Each position tracks your share and accrues rewards in real time. Gas is always paid in QMS.

  • Claim rewards anytime, with a clear network fee in QMS
  • Self-custody first: your keys never leave your wallet
