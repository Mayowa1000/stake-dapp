How it works

Four steps to start earning.
  1. Install a wallet (MetaMask, Trust Wallet) and switch it to the test network shown at the top.
  2. Get free test tokens from the project's faucet.
  3. Tap Connect Wallet, enter an amount, and tap Stake. The first time, you approve the token, then confirm the stake.
  4. Rewards build up every block. Tap Claim rewards or unstake whenever you like.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't.
  • Self-custody. Tokens stay under your keys. This page never sees your private key.
  • Withdraw any time. Unstake your tokens without a lock-up.
  • Live numbers. Pool size, APR, your stake and rewards are read straight from the chain.
  • Open and simple. One static page, no backend, no sign-up.

FAQ

Good to know.

Is the APR guaranteed? No. It is an estimate from the current reward rate and total staked, and it changes as people join or leave.

Can I use real funds? No. This is unofficial and unaudited. Use test tokens only.

The Connect button does nothing. Your browser has no wallet. Open this page inside your wallet app's browser, or use Chrome with the MetaMask extension.
